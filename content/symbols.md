# Recurring symbols (the owner's rule 5)

A concept that comes back from lesson to lesson and from book to book is always drawn with the
**same** symbol, so that each picture adds to the same memory instead of starting a new one. A bad
symbol spoils every later picture, so this list grows slowly and carefully:

- A symbol is a **concrete thing** that is easy to draw and impossible to mistake for something else.
- It is an **English sound-alike** of the term (atropine → a TROPHY), or, when none is good, the
  thing the term literally names or does (corona = a crown; a duct).
- It sticks at first sight. If it needs explaining twice, it is not good enough.
- **The owner approves every symbol before a picture uses it.** Status `proposed` means waiting for
  that approval; `approved` means it may be used in every book. Never invent a second symbol for a
  concept listed here: propose the change here and ask.
- Each scene still has its own setting and colours (the book's theme); only the symbol stays.

## The heart's interior: the HART Hotel (one picture-map for every intracardiac lesson)

A narrow old hotel seen cut open like a doll's house, a stag's head (a *hart* = heart) over its door.

| Part | Drawn as | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Right heart | the **blue** wing, on the viewer's left | blue = venous blood; left of the picture as in every anatomical diagram |
| Left heart | the **red** wing, on the viewer's right | red = arterial blood |
| Atria | the upper floor: two glass-roofed **atrium** lobbies | the word itself |
| Ventricles | the lower floor: engine rooms with huge **vent**ilation fans and thick brick walls | VENT-ricle |
| Septum | the party wall between the blue and the red wing | a dividing wall |
| Superior vena cava | the **chimney** of the blue wing | blood comes down into the right atrium from above |

Places on the upper party wall (top to bottom = a memory palace for the atrial septal defects):

| Defect | Drawn as | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sinus venosus ASD | a white marble **Venus** statue in a hole at the very top, under the chimney | VEN-osus = Venus |
| Secundum ASD | a round **clock with a second hand** in the middle of the wall | SECUND-um = second |
| Ostium primum ASD | a **prime rib** roast in a hole at the very bottom, beside the valve doors | PRIM-um = prime |
| Coronary sinus ASD | a **sink with a crown** (corona) set in the blue lobby's floor | corona + sinus = crown + sink |
