explanation:
Dialysis-dependent renal failure is a contraindication to mechanical circulatory support: B.
Irreversible end-organ failure means the patient is unlikely to benefit, and kidney failure markedly raises the risk of death after implantation: the shrivelled beans tied to the washing machine.
The need for support of both ventricles is not a contraindication: biventricular devices exist.
Moderate aortic insufficiency is repaired or the valve replaced at the same operation, and a patent foramen ovale is closed, because the device would otherwise worsen regurgitation or open a right-to-left shunt.
Atrial fibrillation is common in these patients and does not prevent support.
Memory hook: two knights, a dripping mouth, a trembling Pinocchio and a cat flap can all be handled; beans tied to the washing machine cannot.
