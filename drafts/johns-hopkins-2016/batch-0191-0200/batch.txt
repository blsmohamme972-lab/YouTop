In the picture, the marionette's metal piranhas gnaw the orca's wall until it bulges into an aneurysm.

Vascular complications belong to the vascular type of Ehlers-Danlos syndrome (type IV), caused by COL3A1 mutations; Loeys-Dietz syndrome comes from mutations of the TGF-beta receptors.
Turner syndrome is a chromosomal disorder (45,X) and Noonan syndrome is autosomal dominant, so neither is autosomal recessive.
Memory hook: the marionette's metal piranhas chew the orca's wall.

explanation:
In a bicuspid aortic valve, the cusps and the media of the ascending aorta arise from the same neural crest cells (printed "medial crest"), so one developmental fault weakens both: C.
In the picture, the bicycle and the orca's wall above it carry the same crest: cut from the same cloth.
That is why the ascending aorta can keep dilating even after the valve has been replaced, the bubble that still grows over the tissue box.
In the aortic media of these patients fibrillin-1 is reduced and smooth muscle cells die by apoptosis; apoptosis is described in the aortic wall, not in the valve leaflets.
About one in ten first-degree relatives also has a bicuspid valve, so relatives are screened with echocardiography.
Memory hook: one crest on the bicycle and on the wall above it: the same cloth, the same weakness.

Takayasu arteritis mainly narrows and blocks the aorta and its branches; aneurysms are less common.
Mycotic aneurysms are usually saccular and focal, not diffuse and fusiform, and giant cell arteritis does raise the risk of a thoracic aortic aneurysm.
Memory hook: Morgan's hatchet pops the bubble: in Behcet disease, rupture kills.
