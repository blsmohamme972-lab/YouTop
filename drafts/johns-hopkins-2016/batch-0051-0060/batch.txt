«قتلت هذا السرطان قبل عشر سنوات، وما عاد إليّ منذ ذلك اليوم.»

source: مونكي دي لوفي
explanation:
Lung transplantation is withheld when another condition would ruin the result.
Uncorrectable coronary artery disease, significant left ventricular dysfunction, renal failure and current tobacco use are contraindications.
A previous malignancy is acceptable after a long disease-free interval, usually at least 5 years for most cancers, so 10 years free of disease is not a contraindication: A is the answer.

explanation:

Prostaglandin is injected into the pulmonary artery before the aortic cross-clamp, not after.
At the end the lungs are kept moderately inflated, not deflated, when the trachea is stapled and divided.
The acceptable ischaemic time is about 6 to 8 hours, not 8 to 12.

«لا أستطيع أن أعطي درجةً لما لا أراه!»

source: الدكتور هيلولوك
explanation:

Acute rejection is graded A0 to A4 (minimal is A1, severe is A4), and airway inflammation B0 to B2R (low grade B1R, high grade B2R).
Airway inflammation that cannot be graded is BX: D is the answer.
Chronic airway rejection, bronchiolitis obliterans, is recorded as C0 (absent) or C1 (present), not graded as minimal.

وعند أسفلها، ربط عنقها برباط كعنق الصرّة.
«هذا الرباط يحرس قدري؛ لا يخرج منها شيء إلى الأعلى.» قال سانجي.

source: سانجي
explanation:
The pressure of the lower oesophageal sphincter comes from two parts working together.
The circular smooth muscle of the distal oesophagus provides the intrinsic sphincter, and the striated muscle of the right crus of the diaphragm squeezes it from outside: E is the answer.
Neither part works alone, and the longitudinal muscle does not contribute to the sphincter pressure.
