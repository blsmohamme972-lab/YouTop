explanation:
Coronary artery bypass grafting is not an appropriate operation for an anomalous coronary artery running between the aorta and pulmonary artery in a child: E.
At rest the native artery carries normal flow, so a graft competes with it and tends to close, and a child would outgrow it.

Unroofing the intramural segment is the most common repair, with creation of a neo-ostium, coronary translocation and pulmonary artery translocation as alternatives.

source: مثل عربيّ
explanation:
A coronary fistula can cause either a left-to-right or a left-to-left shunt: D, as corrected (the book prints right-to-left).
Most fistulas are congenital and arise from a normal coronary artery; most end in the right side of the heart, the right ventricle most often, then the right atrium and the pulmonary artery, a left-to-right shunt.
A fistula ending in a left chamber returns arterial blood to the left heart, a left-to-left shunt that loads the ventricle like aortic regurgitation; it never makes a right-to-left shunt.
The feeding artery is dilated and tortuous proximal to the fistula, not distal to it, and most patients have no symptoms and are found by a continuous murmur rather than angina.
In the picture, one fist pours the crown's water into the blue engine room, the other into the red one.
Memory hook: a coronary fist always punches out from the left.
