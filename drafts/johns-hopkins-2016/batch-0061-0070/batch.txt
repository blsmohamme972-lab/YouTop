explanation:
Oesophageal duplication cysts arise from abnormal budding of the foregut early in development.
They make up about 5 to 10 percent of mediastinal cysts: B is the answer.

They are not considered premalignant, though they are usually removed.

explanation:

وعلى الأرض مضخّة إطفاء حمراء مرميّة جانبًا، لم تنفع شيئًا.

explanation:
Antireflux surgery is indicated for reflux that fails or cannot tolerate medical therapy, for complications such as severe oesophagitis, and for a large hiatal hernia.
Barrett's oesophagus with high-grade dysplasia is not an indication for a fundoplication: it needs endoscopic resection and ablation, or oesophagectomy: D is the answer.
A fundoplication controls reflux but does not reliably make dysplasia regress.
