explanation:
Balloon atrial septostomy is not a correct treatment strategy for a critically cyanotic neonate with tetralogy: D.
The problem in tetralogy is too little blood reaching the lungs; tearing the atrial septum, which helps mixing in transposition, does not add any.
The right steps are prostaglandin E1 to keep the duct open, intubation and ventilation when needed, and prompt intervention: a systemic-to-pulmonary shunt, early complete repair, or a stent in the duct or the outflow tract.
