quote: «عيناي تكادان تقفزان من الدهشة... مع أنّه ليس لي عينان! يو هو هو هو!»

explanation:
The triangle of Koch is bounded by the coronary sinus ostium, the septal leaflet of the tricuspid valve and the tendon of Todaro, with the AV node at its apex; the right coronary artery is not part of it: E.
In the picture, the sink, the trident's inner prong and the taut rope draw the triangle, and the knot sits at its apex.
The right coronary artery, the rainbow, runs in the right atrioventricular groove outside the triangle, though in most hearts it gives the small artery that feeds the AV node.
Stitches inside the triangle, during tricuspid, septal or atrial surgery, risk complete heart block.
Memory hook: sink, prong and rope make the triangle, the knot sits on top, and the rainbow stays outside.

source: مونكي دي لوفي
explanation:
A vena contracta width above 0.7 cm, the narrow neck of the jet measured just above the trident, is a major Doppler criterion of severe tricuspid regurgitation: A.
The vena contracta is the narrowest part of the regurgitant jet as it leaves the valve, and its width follows the size of the leak.
Colour Doppler, the police lights, measures it directly; other signs of severe TR are a large jet area and reversed systolic flow in the hepatic veins.
Annular dilatation, septal movement, right ventricular function and pulmonary hypertension describe the heart around the valve, not the leak itself.
Memory hook: shine the police lights on the jet and measure its neck: wider than 7 means severe.

explanation:
Tricuspid regurgitation with a pulmonary artery systolic pressure below 60 mm Hg is not, on its own, an indication for tricuspid surgery: E, the fifth sailor with a small leak and a calm gauge.
Severe regurgitation in a patient having mitral surgery should be repaired at the same operation, the first sailor.
So should severe symptomatic primary regurgitation and severe regurgitation from diseased leaflets, the broken trident and the bent prongs.
At mitral surgery, even lesser regurgitation is repaired when the annulus is dilated or there is pulmonary hypertension, the wide collar and the gauge in the red.
Memory hook: four sailors go to theatre; the fifth, with a small leak and a gauge under 60, goes back to sea.

explanation:
For mitral and tricuspid repair with a graft to the PDA, the order is: distal coronary anastomosis, mitral repair, proximal anastomosis, tricuspid repair: B.
The distal anastomosis to the posterior descending artery, the sapphire chain tied to the back spike, comes first, while the arrested heart can still be lifted to reach it.
The mitral repair follows under the same cross-clamp, and the proximal anastomosis is sewn before the clamp, the X-tongs, comes off.
The tricuspid valve, on the right side, can be repaired last with the heart beating again, the fans turning, which shortens the ischaemic time.
Memory hook: back spike, mitre, top of the chain, and the trident last with the fans turning.

«وإن عاد الطوق فتمدّد، خسرت رهانك!» قال فرانكي.

source: مقدّمة ون بيس (الراوي)
explanation:
The DeVega suture annuloplasty is acceptable for functional tricuspid regurgitation when the loading conditions are expected to improve after the operation: D.
Functional regurgitation comes from a dilated annulus, the collar stretched by the swollen right ventricle, and it often lessens once the left-sided lesion is corrected and the pulmonary pressure falls, the gauge's needle dropping.
The DeVega technique narrows the annulus with a purse-string suture, the gambler's string of casino lights, a bet that the load will improve; when it will not, a ring annuloplasty is more durable.
A mechanical valve is not preferred in the tricuspid position, where the slow flow invites thrombosis, the beaver building on the robot.
Ring annuloplasty does not cause more endocarditis, and a small leak after bicuspidization does not by itself demand replacement.
Memory hook: the Las Vegas gambler bets on falling pressure and ties his string of lights round the collar.
