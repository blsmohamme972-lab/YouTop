quote: «إن التقينا يومًا من جديد، أتنادونني رفيقتكم؟»
source: نفرتاري فيفي
explanation:
In TGA the effective systemic blood flow is equal to the amount of anatomic left-to-right shunting: B.
The two circulations run in parallel: oxygenated blood circulates from the left ventricle to the lungs and back, and only the part that crosses to the right side through an ASD, VSD or ductus reaches the body.
The effective pulmonary and systemic flows are equal, so survival depends on mixing; with an intact septum, prostaglandin and often a balloon septostomy are needed.
The left ventricle, pumping against low pulmonary pressure, loses its ability to support the systemic circulation after a few weeks unless a VSD or outflow obstruction keeps its pressure up.
In the picture, only the red water that crosses the little bridges reaches the city.
Memory hook: only what crosses the bridge feeds the body.
