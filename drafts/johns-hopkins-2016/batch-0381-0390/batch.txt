Remodelling techniques should be considered obsolete for pediatric patients with connective tissue disorders: A.

Replacing the root does not end the disease, since the rest of the aorta stays at risk, and Ehlers-Danlos patients carry a much higher surgical risk because of fragile tissues.

ALCAPA is the most common cause of myocardial infarction in childhood: B.
The left coronary artery, most often arising from the posterior-facing sinus of the pulmonary artery, receives low-pressure, desaturated blood; as pulmonary pressure falls in the first weeks, perfusion fails and infarction follows.
