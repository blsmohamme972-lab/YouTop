explanation:
Patients undergoing gadolinium-enhanced MRI are at risk of nephrogenic systemic fibrosis (NSF) if they have renal failure: D.
Gadolinium is cleared by the kidneys; when they fail, it stays in the body for a long time and can trigger fibrosis of the skin, the joints and internal organs.
The skin thickens and hardens like wood, and contractures can stiffen the joints; the disease can be disabling and even fatal.
The risk is highest with severe chronic kidney disease (an eGFR under 30), acute kidney injury and dialysis; there contrast is avoided or a safer agent is used at the lowest dose.

Memory hook: shrivelled beans keep the gold dust, and the skin turns to wood.

Valvular regurgitation is best quantified with velocity-encoded MRI: B.

The regurgitant volume and fraction follow directly, which makes MRI a reference method when echo is uncertain.
Delayed enhancement shows scar, cine (steady-state free precession) shows motion and size, T2 shows oedema and first-pass imaging shows perfusion; none measures flow.
