في أرض وانو، تمرّ أنابيب الدولارات الخضراء في ينبوع حارّ يتصاعد منه البخار.

«ستتحجّر كلّها، أو خمسها على الأقلّ، وينسدّ الطريق إلى الأبد!» قال أوسوب مذعورًا.
لكنّ الماء الحارّ أذاب أكثر الكرات كالشّمع الأسود، في أسابيع قليلة.
ولم يبق من كلّ مئة إلّا ثلاث أو أربع أو خمس، تحجّرت رماديّةً والتصقت بجدران الأنابيب، فصعد عدّاد الضغط في أنبوبها إلى الأحمر.

quote: «أنا أودن... ولدت لأغلى!»

explanation:
About 3 to 5 percent of patients with an acute pulmonary embolism go on to develop chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH): D.
In most patients the clot dissolves within weeks, the bowling balls melting in the warm spring.
In a few it does not resolve but organises into fibrous tissue fixed to the arterial wall, the grey stones, and the pulmonary vascular resistance and pressure rise: the gauge climbing into the red.
Many patients with CTEPH have no known history of an acute embolism, so the disease is often found late.
Memory hook: of every hundred balls, only three to five turn to stone.

«خمسون؟ رقم صغير! تسعة منهم سيبلغون القمّة سالمين!» قال أوسوب.
فمضت ثلاث سنوات، وتمزّقت أوراق ثلاث رزنامات.

source: رورونوا زورو
explanation:
Once the mean pulmonary artery pressure exceeds 50 mm Hg in chronic thromboembolic disease, about 90 percent of untreated patients die within 3 years: E.
In the picture, of ten climbers whose gauges read above 50, nine are coffins three years later.
The right ventricle fails under the rising pressure, and anticoagulation alone cannot relieve the fixed obstruction.
This poor outlook is why pulmonary endarterectomy is offered as soon as the disease is found to be operable.
Memory hook: a gauge over 50 leaves one climber of ten after three years.

explanation:
Pulmonary angiography is the gold standard for diagnosing CTEPH and for judging whether the disease can be removed surgically: C.
In the picture, only the photographer's magenta dye shows every web, band and blocked branch in the money pipes.
A ventilation-perfusion scan, the two-coloured map, is the best screening test: a normal scan practically rules CTEPH out, but it cannot show where the obstructions lie.
CT angiography (the cat) and echocardiography (the bat) can miss webs and bands in the smaller branches, and pulmonary function tests (the brass horn) do not make the diagnosis.
Memory hook: the photographer's picture is the final judge.

explanation:

Anticoagulation, diuretics and pulmonary vasodilators cannot dissolve organised fibrous clot, the stones that do not fear the bottles, and waiting six months only lets the right ventricle fail further.
Declining treatment would leave a patient whose outlook without surgery is very poor.
Memory hook: the stones do not fear bottles; the scraper takes them out.
