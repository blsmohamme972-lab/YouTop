== 49
source: مثل عربيّ
explanation:
After mediastinal radiation, heart failure with a left ventricular end-diastolic pressure above the right, no ventricular interdependence and concordant ventricular pressures points to restrictive cardiomyopathy, not constrictive pericarditis.
In restriction the stiff myocardium itself limits filling, so both ventricles rise in pressure together, and the left is often higher.
In constriction the rigid pericardium couples the ventricles: their diastolic pressures are equal, and with breathing one rises as the other falls (discordance).
Little respiratory variation on Doppler also favours restriction.
Radiation can cause either, or both, which is why these signs matter: pericardiectomy helps constriction but not restriction.
