explanation:
For CABG and mitral valve replacement in an obese diabetic who has had a long stay in another hospital, the best prophylaxis is a first-generation cephalosporin plus a glycopeptide: cefazolin plus vancomycin.
A long hospital stay raises the chance of colonisation with methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus, which cefazolin does not cover.
Vancomycin alone is weaker against methicillin-sensitive staphylococci and covers no Gram-negative organisms, so it is added to cefazolin rather than replacing it.
