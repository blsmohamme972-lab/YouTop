explanation:
In a patient with chronic kidney disease and a potassium of 5.6 having on-pump CABG, the best way to manage the electrolytes during the operation is zero-balanced ultrafiltration.
The ultrafilter on the bypass circuit removes plasma water with its potassium, and an equal volume of potassium-free balanced solution is given back, so the fluid balance stays at zero.
This lowers the potassium and clears other small molecules without drying the patient out.
Continuous haemofiltration is an ICU therapy, HTK cardioplegia adds a large volume without removing the potassium already in the blood, and glucose with insulin shifts potassium only for a short time.

== 68
source: مثل عربيّ
explanation:
A dissection of the descending aorta seen on TEE soon after a Seldinger aortic cannulation, with a normal-looking insertion site and an intact arch, is best explained by the cannula's jet.
The book's answer is to change to a short dispersion-type cannula at the same site.
An end-hole cannula aimed down the aorta sends a high-velocity jet against the wall, which can raise an intimal flap downstream.
A short dispersion cannula spreads the flow sideways at low velocity, removing the jet while keeping antegrade flow into the true lumen.
Switching to femoral cannulation would perfuse a dissected descending aorta retrogradely and could extend the false lumen, and an arch replacement is not needed when the arch is intact.

== 69
source: مثل عربيّ
explanation:
In a patient with cold agglutinin disease, the safest plan for heart surgery is warm cardioplegia with systemic normothermia.
Cold agglutinins are antibodies that clump red cells, and can destroy them, when the blood is cooled below their thermal range.
Cold cardioplegia or systemic cooling can therefore cause agglutination in the coronary vessels, haemolysis and thrombosis; keeping the blood and the heart warm avoids triggering them.
If cooling cannot be avoided, the antibody's thermal range is measured first, and plasmapheresis or a crystalloid flush of the coronaries may be used.

Memory hook: sticky doughnuts in the cold: keep everything warm.
