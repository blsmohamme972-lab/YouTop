Beside him the cut-open HART Hotel, the green crown's spikes clean with no tar: a long sausage-shaped balloon swells in the orca's belly; the mitre's leaf with a little anteater on it flaps loose like a torn sail, and a flash flood bursts back through it into a small red lobby that has never stretched, flooding up into the money sacks, whose dollar bills drip soggy. In the centre, in the strongest light: a harp-maker strings shining new silver strings onto the anteater's leaf, holding it down, while the physician al-Razi sets a brass collar ring round the doorway. A triangle cut in the leaf with a little possum clinging to it is waved away.
