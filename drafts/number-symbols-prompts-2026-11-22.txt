Picture prompts for the number characters (optional; 2026-11-22).
In lessons a number is a small drawing: a tattoo, a painting on a wall or a design on an object (the owner).
These show each character as that small emblem, and the last one shows 5.5 as a tattoo. Lessons do not need them:
a lesson's own prompt describes the numbers it uses (content/symbols.md, "Numbers").
