explanation:

Myasthenia gravis does not worsen the outcome, debulking is not the mainstay, and the diagnosis is made by core or surgical biopsy rather than fine-needle aspiration.

explanation:

The mediastinal space arises from both of these mesenteries together: B is the answer.

The lungs and pericardium do not arise from the dorsal mesoesophagus, and bronchogenic cysts come from abnormal budding of the ventral foregut.

Paragangliomas can secrete catecholamines; they are not nonfunctional by definition.
