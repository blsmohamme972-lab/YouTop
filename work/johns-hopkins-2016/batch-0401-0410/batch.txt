The atrial septal defect or patent foramen ovale is usually closed, sometimes leaving a small fenestration, never enlarged.

The right common carotid artery is the preferred site for arterial cannulation in neonates and infants on ECMO: A.
It is large enough for a cannula in small babies and is reached through a short neck incision, with the venous cannula placed alongside through the right internal jugular vein into the right atrium.

Reconstruction of the carotid artery after decannulation has not been shown to improve long-term neurologic outcome.
