Patients with connective tissue disorders have a higher risk of recurrence after repair.

وفي وسطه كيس من الدولارات، ملفوف بجلد فضّيّ رقيق لامع.

explanation:
The visceral pleura covers the lung and dips into its fissures.
It has no somatic sensory nerves, so it is insensitive to pain: C is the answer.
The parietal pleura has somatic innervation from the intercostal and phrenic nerves, and it is the source of pleuritic pain.
Venous blood from the visceral pleura drains into the pulmonary veins, and from the parietal pleura into the systemic veins.
