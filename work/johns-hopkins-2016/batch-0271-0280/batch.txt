explanation:
The maximum amount of whole body radiation dose per year is 5 rems: D.
This is the occupational limit for staff, 50 mSv; in the picture, five glowing rams (REMs) pass the gate each year and the sixth is stopped.
Cancer is a stochastic effect with no threshold: the risk rises with any dose, which is why every flash counts.
Exposure falls with the square of the distance, so standing twice as far gives a quarter of the dose, not half.
Pregnant staff may keep working with a lead apron and a second badge at the waist; the limit for the fetus is 0.5 rem over the whole pregnancy.
The lifetime limit is about 1 rem times the age in years, not 100 rems.
Memory hook: five rams a year through the gate.
