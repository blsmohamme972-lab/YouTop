في صباح هادئ، كان فم الحوت فوق غرفة المحرّكات الحمراء مرتخيًا قليلًا، يقطر منه الماء راجعًا إلى الغرفة.

ثمّ نظرت إلى صاحب الفندق المطمئنّ وقالت:
quote: «رأى من أنينه أنّه عليل القلب؛ الجسد سليم، وهو أسير القلب.»
source: المثنوي، الكتاب الأول
explanation:
In chronic aortic insufficiency, the timing of operation is guided by symptoms and by the size and function of the left ventricle.
A patient with moderate insufficiency and a normal left ventricular end-systolic dimension can be managed medically and followed with echocardiography: A is the answer.
Cardiomegaly on the chest film, symptoms of heart failure, or an end-systolic dimension as large as 70 mm all point to operation.
An end-systolic dimension above about 50 to 55 mm warns that the ventricle is beginning to fail, and the valve should be replaced before the damage is permanent.

explanation:
The question asks which statements are not true.
Tricuspid regurgitation is most often functional, from annular dilatation secondary to left-sided disease and pulmonary hypertension, not from abnormal leaflets, so statement C is not true.

explanation:

Hoarseness, from compression of the left recurrent laryngeal nerve by a large left atrium (Ortner's syndrome), also belongs to mitral disease.
So A and D are the answer.

في البهو الأحمر المضاء كالكاتدرائيّة، جلس الأسقف وعلى رأسه تاج ثقيل.
كان تاجه القديم قد تحوّل حجرًا أبيض صلبًا متشقّقًا، لا ينثني ولا ينفتح.

explanation:
Most regurgitant mitral valves can be repaired, including those with ruptured chordae or a dilated annulus.
Extensive calcification of the leaflets makes repair unreliable, so it is a relative indication for replacement: A is the answer.
Mitral regurgitation itself, chordal rupture of the anterior leaflet and annular dilatation are all usually repairable.
Repair, when possible, is preferred, because it preserves ventricular function and avoids lifelong anticoagulation.
