A retrograde type A dissection two days after TEVAR is a surgical emergency, best treated by replacing the ascending aorta and the whole arch, joined directly to the stent graft.
The new tear usually starts at the proximal end of the stent graft and climbs back into the ascending aorta.

More TEVAR further up does not treat an ascending dissection, and medical therapy alone carries a high risk of rupture and tamponade.
