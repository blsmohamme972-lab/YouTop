== 96
# 1 a glassblower's long iron blowpipe                          = the trachea
# 2 two enormous swollen blue wineskins lying across the blowpipe and squeezing it flat = huge, tortuous pulmonary arteries compressing the trachea (ANSWER)
# 3 the glassblower purple in the face, his glass bubble sagging = severe respiratory distress
# 4 a blue doorway with empty hinges, its door gone, from which the wineskins swell out = the absent pulmonary valve behind such arteries
# 5 a mason pointing at a hole in the wall and at the thick wall around it, nobody listening = the septal defect and the thick right ventricle are there but are not the cause
# 6 an iron ring hanging unused on a nail                      = it is not a vascular ring
title: Fallot with huge pulmonary arteries: the trachea is squeezed
titleAr: فالو مع شرايين رئوية هائلة: الرغامى مضغوطة

must: two huge swollen blue wineskins lie across the long iron blowpipe and flatten it; the glassblower's glass bubble at the end of the pipe is sagging; the blue doorway behind has empty hinges and no door

CENTRE, dominant and larger than life: two enormous swollen cobalt-blue wineskins, each bigger than a man, lie across the middle of the blowpipe and squeeze it flat between them; the glassblower's face is purple, his eyes wide, one hand clawing at his collar. BEHIND, upper right: a blue doorway with empty hinges and no door, from which the necks of the two wineskins swell out. LEFT: a mason points at a hole in a thick brick wall, and nobody looks at him. On a nail above him hangs an iron ring, unused.

# 4 a boy who has pushed an ox-bladder on a long cane inside the hose and blows it up exactly at the pinch, which is widening = balloon dilatation (ANSWER)
# 5 a fireman with knife and needle made to sit and wait on a bench = surgery is the second choice

CENTRE, dominant and larger than life: a boy has unscrewed the nozzle end, pushed a long cane up inside the hose and blows into it with full cheeks; exactly at the pinch a round pink ox-bladder is swelling inside the hose, seen through the wet canvas, and the pinch is widening. RIGHT: a fireman with a big knife and a threaded needle sits on a bench, made to wait, arms folded.
