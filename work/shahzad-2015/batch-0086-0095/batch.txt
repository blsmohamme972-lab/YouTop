== 86
# 1 a wide green country pipe joined to a narrow red town pipe = a vein graft sewn to a coronary artery
# 2 the wide pipe cut open: its whole inside has grown a smooth, even lining of grey felt, so its bore now equals the narrow pipe's = diffuse intimal hyperplasia: a remodelling that matches the lumen to the artery (ANSWER)
# 3 twelve notches on the bench                              = universal by one year
# 4 a boy passing the same rod through both pipes            = the lumens match; smooth and diffuse, not progressive
# 5 on a shelf, for later years: a pipe with rough rusty lumps = atherosclerosis comes later
# 6 a crooked, leaking joint thrown in the corner             = technical error is the cause in the first weeks, not this
title: Vein graft in its first year: intimal hyperplasia
titleAr: الطُّعم الوريدي في عامه الأول: فرط تنسّج البطانة
tags: cardiac/coronary d:pathology q:recall l:1 x:high-yield
palette: workshop wood browns, one leaf-green pipe and one red pipe, soft grey felt, morning light on the bench
must: the green pipe is sawn open lengthwise and its inside is lined all round with an even layer of smooth grey felt; the boy holds one rod that fits both the green pipe and the red pipe
scene: a plumber's workshop in morning light. On the bench, CENTRE, dominant and larger than life: a wide leaf-green pipe joined end to end to a narrow red pipe; the green pipe has been sawn open lengthwise, and its whole inside is lined with a smooth, even layer of grey felt that has grown there, leaving a channel exactly as narrow as the red pipe's. A boy slides one straight rod through both, and it fits both alike; the old plumber watches with raised eyebrows. Twelve notches are cut in a row along the edge of the bench. ON A SHELF behind: a pipe studded inside with rough rusty lumps. IN THE CORNER on the floor: a crooked joint, leaking.

== 94
# 1 a hall with a wide gap low in the wall between its two sides, a carpenter fitting a patch over it = a partial atrioventricular canal: closing the primum defect with a patch
# 2 a small drain opening at the lower edge of the gap          = the coronary sinus, displaced inferiorly
# 3 a brass bell-box with its wire, sitting between the drain and the ridge of the floor = the atrioventricular node, between the coronary sinus and the ventricular crest
# 4 the carpenter's line of nails swinging wide round the FAR side of the drain, so that the drain ends up on the left of the patch and no nail goes near the bell-box = the sinus is left on the left side to avoid conduction block (ANSWER)
# 5 a thin thread of blue water now trickling into the red side = a small right-to-left shunt, minimal desaturation, well tolerated
# 6 a chalk line that ran straight through the bell-box, crossed out = the direct suture line would cause heart block
title: Partial AV canal: the coronary sinus is left on the left to spare the conduction tissue
titleAr: القناة الأذينية البطينية الجزئية: يُترك الجيب الإكليلي يسارًا لحماية التوصيل
tags: cardiac/congenital d:technique d:anatomy q:recall l:3
palette: polished oak and brass, the right side of the hall blue-lit, the left side red-lit, white chalk lines on the floor
must: the row of nails curves round the far side of the small drain so that the drain lies on the red left side of the patch; a brass bell-box sits untouched between the drain and a ridge in the floor; a straight chalk line through the bell-box is crossed out
scene: a hall seen from above, its right half lit blue and its left half lit red, divided by a wall that has a wide gap low down. CENTRE, dominant and larger than life: a carpenter kneels fitting a pale wooden patch over the gap; at the lower edge of the gap is a small round drain, and between the drain and a raised ridge in the floor sits a brass bell-box with its wire, gleaming. His row of nails does not run straight: it swings wide round the far side of the drain, so that the drain lies on the red left side of the patch and no nail comes near the bell-box.
